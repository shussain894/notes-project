# Jumu'ah

**Muhammad:**
- Last ummah but will enter Jannah first. Allah guided us to the right day.

**Muhammad:**

- Best of days is Friday, Adam created, spirit blown into him. The hour will come on this day, indeed there is an hour where if you ask, Allah will give.

**Salaf** used to like working on this day.

**Ibn Hajar:**
- 45 opinions about sa'at al istijaba.
  1. Between Asr & Maghrib
  2. **Umar:** When Imam comes out with salah (Sahih Muslim)

**Khiraqi:**
- Tabi'een got together and didn't leave until agreeing it's between Asr & Maghrib on Friday.

Greatest deed on Friday is the salah. Scholars say it is called Jumu'ah because of the root letters.

**Muhammad:**
- Whoever leaves 3 Jumu'ah, Allah puts a seal on his heart, & he becomes of the ghaafileen.

Time of Jumu'ah according to most scholars comes after time of zawwal.

**Ahmad:**
- Praying before zawwal is acceptable.

**Companions:**
- We used to pray & when going home saw the shadows, there was no shade (meaning it was after zawwal).

**Khiraqi:**
- Imam Ahmad never encouraged the salah before zawwal, he just said it is not invalid.

**Ibn ul Qayyim:**
- You can critically degrade a hadith if it has exaggerated reward.

**Muhammad:**
- Whoever does ghusl, & comes early & he walks & he doesn't ride & he sits close to the Imam & he listens, for every step he gets one year reward of fasting & praying.

**Abu Hurairah narrates from Muhammad:**
- When it is a Friday, the angels stand at the gate of the mosque and keep on writing the names of the persons coming to the mosque in succession according to their arrivals. The example of the one who enters the mosque in the earliest hour is that of one offering a camel (in sacrifice). The one coming next is like one offering a cow and then a ram and then a chicken and then an egg respectively. When the Imam comes out (for Jumua prayer) they (i.e. angels) fold their papers and listen to the Khutbah.

**Abdullah Ibn Masood:**
- went early to jumu'ah, someone said "we are the 4th", he said: "that is not too bad, I heard Muhammad say we will be close to Allah according to how close you are to the khateeb."

**Uthmaan** spoke during the adhaan.
**Uthmaan** implemented a 2nd adhaan when Madinah got too big.

Scholars say seal of the hearts is due to missing the khutbah


- **Muhammad** used to lean on a palm tree and give the khutbah. **Sahaba** built a minbar. Palm tree weeped as it used to listen to the dhikr of Allah (khutbah) — **Muhammad**.

**Muhammad:**
- If a person tells someone to be quiet, he has said foul speech & has lost the reward of Jumu'ah.

Nothing wrong with addressing the khateeb.

**Jabir:**
- He would stand in anger, red eyes, and shout. (Sahih Muslim)

If person gave khutbah sitting, still valid.
To see a person's understanding of deen, see he will shorten the khutbah & lengthen the salah.

**Khiraqi:**
- Khateeb can make dua for anyone he wants.

**Imam Ahmad:**
- Used to make dua for the mu'tazzilat.

**Ahmad:** 
- If I had one dua accepted, it'd be for the leaders, if they are upright, then the rest will be ok.

Sitting in between khutbah is mustahab.
> **Shafi'i** said wajib
> Not invalid if you don't sit.

- If you catch 1 rakah, you have caught jumu'ah.
- If you miss it (after 2nd rakah), you pray and continue it as dhuhr.
- If you catch it after 2nd rakah and have niyyah for jumu'ah and only pray 2, you must pray dhuhr again.
- If asr comes in whilst praying, you have caught jumu'ah.

- **Imam Ahmad & Imam Shafi'i:** Pray tahiyatul-masjid.
- **Malik & Hanafi:** Don’t have to pray.
    - Hadith of man climbing shoulders, **Muhammad** told him to sit down as he was harming others. (However, something missing: had he prayed or not?)

**Khiraqi:** 
- If there's not 40 men (sane) in a town, they don’t have to pray jumu'ah.
- **Muhammad never prayed jumu'ah in Makkah.**

- First jumu'ah had **40 (adult) men**, so this became a condition.
    - It must be a town.

**Ahmad:** 
- 3 people are enough.

- 12 is enough also; Surat ul-Jumu'ah v9-11 revealed when — story when men rushed to the caravan of Dihyah bin Khalifah (not Muslim yet) when he returned from Syria, Muslims were hungry and rushed to the marketplace, hadith of **Jabir bin Abdullah:** only 12 people remained

- **Muhammad sent Mus'ab bin Umayr** to Madinah to give da'wah. He led the first jumu'ah.

- Conditions of **Ibn Qudamah:**
    1. Town
    2. 40
    3. Men
    4. Reached puberty
    5. Intellect
    6. Muslim

- **Abu Darr:** There aren't 3 that pray in jama'ah that shaytaan doesn't overcome them.

- **Muhammad:** No jumu'ah salah wajib upon the musafir.
- **Muhammad: in Final Sermon** He prayed silent 2 and combined with asr.

- **Ibn Qudamah:** Better for musafir or slave to pray jumu'ah.

- **Khiraqi:** If it's wajib for you to pray jumu'ah & you pray dhuhr, you must pray dhuhr again after jumu'ah, as dhuhr can only be made up after jumu'ah.
