# Prayer of the Traveller

Travelling 16 farsakh (4 bareed), 48 hashimi miles, he can shorten it once he leaves his town.  
4 bareed is the opinion of **Abdullah ibn Amr and Ibn Abbas**
safr (root word) = exposed

Ayah in Surah An-nisa says shorten prayer if you fear disbelievers will harm you.
**Ya'la ibn Iyyanah** said to **Umar**: “We don’t fear anymore.”  
**Umar asked Muhammad**: “It is sadaqa from Allah, so accept.”

When something isn’t legislated, you go to the custom at the time.

Distance of 48 miles is from outside of your town.

- **Anas**: **Muhammad** shortened when he went 3 miles.
- **Ali** went just outside Kufa & shortened salah.

There is a view that you can only shorten if going for ibadah.
- **Abdullah ibn Mas’ud**: Only shortened for Hajj & Jihad.

- **Abu Hanifah**: Can shorten for anything.

Can not shorten if you intend for haram.

- **Khiraqi**: If you didn’t intend to shorten salah & salah comes in, you cannot shorten it.

- **Abu Bakr (Hanbali) & Al-Majj (Judge & Ibn Taymiyyah)**: You don’t have to intend anything.

- Fajr & maghrib not shortened. (ijma)

- **Khiraqi**: He can shorten prayer if he wants to, but he should do it.
- **Imam Ahmad**: He can shorten, or pray full.
- **Imam Abu Hanifa and Ibn Taymiyyah**: You must shorten it.
- **Ibn Abbas**: If you pray 4 while travelling, it is like praying 2 at home.

**Khiraqi:**
- When dhuhr starts, pray only dhuhr, pray each salah in its time.
- If he's moving, he can delay or combine.

 **An-Nawawi in Majmoo’ - Al Ghazali:**
- Said consensus to not combine.

- Bringing one salah forward — Jamu’ at-taqdeem — Some scholars say can’t do this

 **Anas:**
- **Muhammad** prayed dhuhr + asr at dhuhr.
- If he started & dhuhr not come in, he prayed dhuhr + asr at asr.

- Can only combine; traveling, sick, fear, rain.

 **Ibn Taymiyyah:**
- Whole point is to remove hardness.

 **Ibn Qudamah in Al-Mughni:**
- If you go masjid under shelter in rain, or live in masjid, you can’t combine.

**Khiraqi:**
- If a person forgets to pray, when travelling then remembered at home:
  - He prays as if he is at home.

- Sunnah to pray behind imam as he prays.
- If a traveller comes late, he still makes it up. — **Abdullah ibn Umar in Sahih Muslim**

 **Muhammad**
- A visitor shouldn't lead them in salah.

## How long can a person shorten for?

 **Imam Ahmad:**
- 21 salawaat.

 **Abdullah Ibn Abbas:**
- If go for 19 days, will shorten as **Muhammad** done that.

**Anas**: 
- **Muhammad** Went Makkah for 10 days & shortened.

**Imam Ahmad:**
- In the above 2, he was in one place for 21 salawaat, then moved.

- If you intend to go for more than 21 salawaat, don't shorten.

- If someone is unsure when they'll leave, may shorten, even forever. Same Sahabah shortened for 6 months.

**Ibn Taymiyyah:**
- Shorten for as long as you’re gone.

 **Aisha:**
- Shorten until you put down provisions + rucksack.
