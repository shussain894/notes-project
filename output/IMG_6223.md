# Nullifiers of Salah

If a pillar is left, salah is invalid.  
If a condition is left, salah is invalid.

If one can't remember, then they're excused in such situation.

*Ibn Abi Auf* — *Muhammad* told him to say tasbeeh, he just took his shahada.

If a person leaves a pillar, and can make it up, they do so and build it up from there.

If one forgets al-fatiha & remembers in next rakah, his salah starts in next rakah.

## Wājibāt

- Don't have to compensate for if behind an imam.
- If any are left intentionally then your salah is invalid.

1. Takbeerat al-intiqal
2. Tasbeeh in ruku
3. Tasbeeh in sujood
4. Tasbeeh - *samiallahuliman hamidah*
5. *Rabbana wa lakal-hamd*
6. *Rabbighfirlee*
7. Middle tashahhud
8. Salah on Muhammad

If one forgets 7 (Middle tashahhud) intentionally, his salah is void.
