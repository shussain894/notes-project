# Sujood as-Sahw

- *Muhammad* was praying 4 rakah
- He stood up for 1st tashahhud
- He signalled that they should get up
- He finished salah
- And made up for it by Sajdah as-sahw

Hadith of *Dhu'l Yadayn* in Sahih Muslim
*Imran ibn Hussain* informed Ibn Sireen that the Prophet saw offered the final tasleem after sajdah as-sahw

## Hanbali - 2 cases where sujood as-sahw is after tasleem

1. Whoever did Salam and some salah remaining, he cuts his salah short, prayed 3, not 4.  
   He will make up the rak'ah or missed salah, then tasleem then sujood as-sahw.

   *Abu Hurairah:*
   *Muhammad* prayed 2 (forgetfully).  
   *Dhu’l-Yadayn* said did you forget or receive wahi (revelation)?  
   He said neither.  
   He prayed the rest, tasleem then sujood.

## Hanafi

- last rak'ah – tasleem once
- Sujood – sit up – sujood
- Tashahhud after tasleem – tashahhud again then tasleem

- Imran ibn Hussain Hadith didn't mention tashahhud, tashahhud only mentioned in Tirmidhi.
- *Ash'hak* - Student of Ibn Sireen (one of narrators) made mistake in narration re: tashahhud.

2. 

   Leading the prayer: If imam has doubt, he does at-taharry (weighs it up).
   *Abdullah bin Masood:* If one of you has doubt in salah, let him look out for what's right then complete salah, then sujood as-sahw after tasleem.

*Sayid Ibn Qudri:*
- If one of you has doubt, he forgets how much prayed, let him build on certainty then remove doubt then do sujood as-sahw before tasleem.

*Imam Az-Zuhri*
- Always take last action of Muhammad.

*Khiraqi*
- The rest are before tasleem.
- If you miss a wajib, sujood is before tasleem.
- If you miss a wajib, you cannot make it up, you carry on.
- If you recite out loud when in quiet prayer. (Do you have the niyyah for the right salah?)

- Increasing a pillar isn't allowed - 3 sujood or  5 ruku'
  His salah is nullified.  
  His sujood as-sahw is before tasleem.

*Muhammad:*
- "If anyone of you forgets then let him do sujood as-sahw."
  - this goes for seven wajibat: adhan, takbeer, shuroot

Scholars say when there’s an increase in salah; sujood is after, decrease in salah; sujood is before
> Like in hadith of *Dhul Yadayn* — when they made the rest of the salah up, they increased the salah.

*Abu Bakr Al-Athram:* Imam Ahmad had this opinion for sujood for after & before.
> Imam Ahmad's main student: genius.
> Ibn Qudamah has this view (nobody had more fiqh in Dimishq for 500 years).
> Imam Malik also said this.

*Khiraqi:*
- If you forget to do sujud as-sahw before tasleem and you remember after tasleem, then do it after the tasleem.
- If you left masjid, then you don't need to.

- If you only do one sujud in every rakah, then remember at the tashahhud, make another sujud in tashahhud (now you have done 2 sajdah's) - That'll be your first rakah; build on that in tashahhud and sujud as-sahw will be after.

*Imam Ahmad* has another opinion: to pray again, he is playing around, his heart was not in it.

*Khiraqi*: If person leaves wajib behind an imam, he doesn't need to do sujud as-sahw.

- If person stands up to complete Salah & imam goes into sujud as-sahw after tashahhud, he continues as he has moved on as imam's salah has finished anyway.

*Khiraqi*: Whoever talks intentionally, his salah is invalid.

*Abdullah ibn Mas'ud*: Before hijrah to Habasha, they used to speak in salah. After, he spoke to *Muhammad* and he didn't reply. After, *Muhammad* said "in salah there is something that preoccupies".

*Ahmad, Maliki, Shafi’i*: Talking forgetfully is overlooked.

*Ibn Qudamah*: If he forgets he's in salah – it’s ok (*Maliki & Shafi’i*).

- If imam stands up for 5th rakah, you stay and complete.  
- If imam ends it early, stand up and complete salah.
