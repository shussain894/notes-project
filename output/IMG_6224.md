# Sujood as-Sahw

- *Muhammad* was praying 4 rakah
- He stood up for 1st tashahhud
- He signalled that they should get up
- He finished salah
- And made up for it by Sajdah as-sahw

Hadith of *Dhu'l Yadayn* in Sahih Muslim
*Imran ibn Hussain* informed Ibn Sireen that the Prophet saw offered the final tasleem after sajdah as-sahw

## Hanbali - 2 cases where sujood as-sahw is after tasleem

1. Whoever did Salam and some salah remaining, he cuts his salah short, prayed 3, not 4.  
   He will make up the rak'ah or missed salah, then tasleem then sujood as-sahw.

   *Abu Hurairah:*
   *Muhammad* prayed 2 (forgetfully).  
   *Dhu’l-Yadayn* said did you forget or receive wahi (revelation)?  
   He said neither.  
   He prayed the rest, tasleem then sujood.

## Hanafi

- last rak'ah – tasleem once
- Sujood – sit up – sujood
- Tashahhud after tasleem – tashahhud again then tasleem

- Imran ibn Hussain Hadith didn't mention tashahhud, tashahhud only mentioned in Tirmidhi.
- *Ash'hak* - Student of Ibn Sireen (one of narrators) made mistake in narration re: tashahhud.

2. 

   Leading the prayer: If imam has doubt, he does at-taharry (weighs it up).
   *Abdullah bin Masood:* If one of you has doubt in salah, let him look out for what's right then complete salah, then sujood as-sahw after tasleem.

*Sayid Ibn Qudri:*
- If one of you has doubt, he forgets how much prayed, let him build on certainty then remove doubt then do sujood as-sahw before tasleem.
