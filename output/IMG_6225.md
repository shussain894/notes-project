*Imam Az-Zuhri*
- Always take last action of Muhammad.

*Khiraqi*
- The rest are before tasleem.
- If you miss a wajib, sujood is before tasleem.
- If you miss a wajib, you cannot make it up, you carry on.
- If you recite out loud when in quiet prayer. (Do you have the niyyah for the right salah?)

- Increasing a pillar isn't allowed - 3 sujood or  5 ruku'
  His salah is nullified.  
  His sujood as-sahw is before tasleem.

*Muhammad:*
- "If anyone of you forgets then let him do sujood as-sahw."
  - this goes for seven wajibat: adhan, takbeer, shuroot

Scholars say when there’s an increase in salah; sujood is after, decrease in salah; sujood is before
> Like in hadith of *Dhul Yadayn* — when they made the rest of the salah up, they increased the salah.

*Abu Bakr Al-Athram:* Imam Ahmad had this opinion for sujood for after & before.
> Imam Ahmad's main student: genius.
> Ibn Qudamah has this view (nobody had more fiqh in Dimishq for 500 years).
> Imam Malik also said this.
