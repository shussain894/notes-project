# Witr

**Khiraqi:**  
- Witr is a single rakah. Qunoot in witr.  
- Separate from other prayers.

**Abdullah Ibn Masood + Scholars of Kufah:**  
- Wajib (emphasised sunnah)  

**Imam Ahmad:**
- It is sunnah, whoever leaves it is an evil person, his testimony should be rejected.

**Muhammad:**
- If you fear the dawn, pray 1 rakah.

**Muhammad:**
- Separate your 2 from 1 with tasleem.

**Khiraqi:**  
- 2 + 1 is liked more.

**Hanafi, Shafi’i, Maliki:**
- 3 together

**Khiraqi:**
- Qunoot before or after ruku.
- No authentic hadith that Muhammad prayed Qunoot in witr.
- Qunoot is sunnah when calamity befalls the muslims.

**Muhammad told 'Ubayy ibn Kab:**
- Lead taraweeh & read Qunoot in last half of ramadan.

**Imam Ahmad:**
- Only do Qunoot in Ramadan.

**Imam Ahmad:**
- People of Makkah made dua after finishing Quran. **Sufyan ibn Uyayanah (imam of Makkah** was there too.

# Taraweeh

**Khiraqi:**  
- 20 rakah

**Aisha:**  
- Muhammad prayed 11 at night.

**Imam Ahmad, Companions of Haneefa, Ali:**
- Better to pray in jama'ah

**Imam Malik & Imam Shafi’i:**  
- Better to pray individually

**Abu Darr' — Muhammad said:**
- Whoever prays with the imam until he finishes, he’ll get reward for standing the whole night.

**Muhammad:**
- Whoever stands the whole of ramadan, all his past sins are forgiven.
