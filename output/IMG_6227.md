*Khiraqi:*
- If his clothes aren't clean, or the place, then repeat the prayer.  
- Scholars say clean clothes/place is a condition.

*Abu Sa'id al-Khudri:*
- To some scholars: If he didn't know and realises after, he doesn't need to repeat.
> Jibraeel came to *Muhammad* and he took off his sandals.

*Shafi'i:* Repeat salah

*Maliki:* Repeat salah if time is still there.

*Khiraqi:* Same applies if he prays in graveyard or toilet.
