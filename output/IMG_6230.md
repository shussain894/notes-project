# When Prayers Are Forbidden

Even in the time when salah is forbidden, a person can do qaza of salah.  
- **Muhammad:** "Whoever forgets Dhuhr or Asr should pray when he remembers."
  - **Hanafi:** Says no.
- **Muhammad:** "Whoever catches one rakah of Fajr or Asr has caught it."


## Three Major Times
1. After Fajr 
2. Zawwal
3. After Asr

## Five Times
1. After Fajr (salah) – until sun rises
2. From when sun has risen until sun rises a spear's length above the ground
3. Zawwal
4. After Asr
5. When sun is about to set until it sets

**Abdullah Ibn Abbas:**  
- Some noble men testified, best of them was Umar. **Muhammad** forbade salah after Fajr & Asr until sun sets.

**Uqbah Ibn Amir**
- **Muhammad** forbade us praying at 3 times & burying the dead:
  1. When sun was rising
  2. Zawwal
  3. When sun is about to set

**Khiraqi:**  
- You can pray your nafl at all 5 times - (1 opinion of Imam Ahmad)

**Imam Malik narrates from Umar**
- He done tawaf, didn't pray, went somewhere else & prayed sunnah.
- **Umar** would hit those who prayed at these times - (Abu Darr, Abu Darda was hit?)

**Khiraqi:**  
- Safer to not pray at these times.

Regular Nawaafil cannot be prayed at these times
> (Ahmad's 100 rak'aat, Abdulgani al-Maqdasi's 100 rak'aat)

Other nawaafil cannot be prayed at 3 major times
- Making up sunan, tahiyyatul masjid, ...

**Muhammad:**  
- O workers of the ka'bah, don't stop anyone praying to this house at any time of day or night!

Ijma with Muslims you can pray janazah after Fajr & Asr.

**Shafii, Ibn Taymiyyah & Ibn Uthimeen:**
- You can pray at these times.

**Imam Ahmad:**
- Was asked about after Fajr where sun is red/yellow, he said he didn't like it, then mentioned **Uqbah ibn Amir** hadith.

**Ibn Taymiyyah**
- You can pray janazah at these times;
- We have 2 general statements, if one has been weakened, take the other one.
> Muhammad prayed after Asr & Fajr

**Imam Abu Haneefa, Imam Malik & Imam Ahmad:**
- Do not pray at these 3 times:
1. After salat ul-Asr until sun has set.
2. After time of Fajr until sun sets - scholars say prohibited time starts at time of fajr, not salatul fajr
3. Zawwal.

**Abdullah ibn Umar, ibn Abbas, Abdullah ibn Masood & Aisha:**
- Prayed witr when they woke up for Fajr.

**Khiraqi:**
- Person won't pray any absolute voluntary salah at any 5 times.

**Ibn Qudamah:**
- I don't know any ikhtilaf.

**Abdullah ibn Umar:**
- I have learnt 10 raka'at of Muhammad. (2 before Dhuhr)

**Ali from Abu Bakr → Muhammad:**
- There isn't a man who sins, & stands up to make wudu, then prays, then makes istighfar but Allah forgives him.
